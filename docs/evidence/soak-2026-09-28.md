## Monitor

`tools/soak-monitor.sh` runs on each router from
`/tmp/mqtt-metrics-soak/monitor.sh` (started with `start-stop-daemon`, pid
in `/tmp/mqtt-metrics-soak/ssd.pid`) and appends a sample every 300 s to
`/tmp/mqtt-metrics-soak/samples.csv`: daemon pid, RSS, CPU ticks, state,
message, publish error and reconnect counters, and new warning/error log
lines. `/tmp` is tmpfs; a router reboot ends the soak.

## Evaluation

Pass criteria: one pid (no restart), state `connected` in every sample, no
publish errors, no warning or error log lines, RSS growth below 10 % after
the first hour.
